# Webtoon / Manhwa Production Script: Episode 4
**Series Working Title:** *The Panopticon: Dynamite City* (पैनोप्टिकॉन: बारूद का साया)  
**Episode 4 Title:** *The Delusion & The Tragic Awakening* (अध्याय 4: भ्रम और खौफनाक सच)  
**Format:** Vertical Scroll Webtoon / Manhwa Standard (9:16 Panels)  
**Visual Style Lock:** 2D Korean Webtoon Manhwa (*Solo Leveling* / *Castle* style, sharp cel-shaded linework, dynamic digital lighting, zero 3D CGI)  
**Tone:** Psychological Thriller, False Relief, Visceral Horror, Heartbreaking Tragedy  
**Total Panels:** 26 Cinematic Panels

## SCENE 1: THE FAREWELL AT THE GLASS & THE AUTOMATIC UNLOCK
**Setting:** Outside and inside the heavy blast door of 14 Old Barracks Road.

#### PANEL 1
* **Asset:** `webnovel_assets/episodes/episode_4/panel_01_gaurav_farewell_at_slit.jpg`
* **Shot Type:** Cinematic Close-Up — The Door Slit
* **Visual Direction:** Outside view focused on the small rectangular glass observation slit of the sealed iron door. Gaurav’s tear-stained face is close to the glass, hand pressed against the cold steel. Inside, through the dark scratched glass, Rahul's silhouette in his olive jacket is fading into dense swirling green Knox gas.
* **Caption (Gaurav - HI):** "कमरे के अंदर राहुल धीरे-धीरे गायब सा हो गया था... मैं जानता था कि अब कुछ नहीं किया जा सकता।"

#### PANEL 2
* **Asset:** `webnovel_assets/episodes/episode_4/panel_02_rahul_zero_countdown_despair.jpg`
* **Shot Type:** Low-Angle Interior — The 00:00 Surrender
* **Visual Direction:** Cut INSIDE the chamber. The digital wall clock flashes **`[ 00:00 ]`** in dark crimson. Toxic pale-green Knox gas covers the entire room. Rahul is collapsed on his knees, clutching his throat, coughing weakly, his eyes closed as he accepts his inevitable death.

#### PANEL 3
* **Asset:** `webnovel_assets/episodes/episode_4/panel_03_voti_delayed_unlock.jpg`
* **Shot Type:** Dynamic Medium Shot — The Door Swings Open!
* **Visual Direction:** Sudden mechanical miracle! The keypad by the door flashes bright neon-green: **`[ VÕTI: ACCEPTED ]`**! The heavy industrial locks disengage with a loud pneumatic hiss, and the massive steel blast door swings OPEN into bright morning sunlight! Rahul looks up in stunned awe.
* **SFX:** *क्लिक-धड़ाम!! (Pneumatic lock disengage)*
