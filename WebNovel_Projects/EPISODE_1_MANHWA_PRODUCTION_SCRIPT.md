# Webtoon / Manhwa Production Script: Episode 1
**Series Working Title:** *The Panopticon: Dynamite City* (पैनोप्टिकॉन: बारूद का साया)  
**Episode 1 Title:** *The Sovereign of Shadow and the ₹200 Note* (अध्याय 1: बारूद का साया और दो सौ का नोट)  
**Format:** Vertical Scroll Webtoon / Manhwa Standard

## PROLOGUE: THE RULE OF SHADOWS & THE THREE TABOOS (प्रस्तावना: गुप्त साये और तीन वर्जित नियम)
**Setting:** Desolate rural village outskirts at the dead of night. Pitch-black void illuminated only by a single distant, buzzing orange sodium streetlight and heavy swirling mist.

### [PROLOGUE BEAT 1: THE FORBIDDEN MARCH]
#### PANEL P01
* **Shot Type:** Low-Angle Tracking Wide Shot (Dynamic Perspective)
* **Visual Direction:** A line of 5–6 young rural children walking in a rigid, terrified single file along an empty dirt path. In the far distance, a single flickering orange sodium streetlamp casts long, distorted shadows. At the head of the line, a 15-year-old teenage boy in a worn cotton kurta and mud-splattered trousers leads them forward, veins bulging on his neck as he barks orders back without turning his head.
* **SFX:** *THUD... STEP... STEP...*
* **Dialogue (Leader Boy - HI):** "अब सब मेरी बात ध्यान से सुनो!"
* **Dialogue (Leader Boy - EN):** "Listen to me carefully, everyone!"

---

#### PANEL P02
* **Shot Type:** High-Angle Creeping Dread / Silhouette Shot
* **Visual Direction:** Looking down from behind the marching children. Emerging from the pitch-black tree line behind them are multiple towering, amorphous black shadow entities with elongated limbs and hollow, featureless forms gliding soundlessly over the ground. Not a single child looks back; their backs are stiff as stone.
* **SFX:** *SHSHSH... (Ominous creeping mist)*

---

#### PANEL P03
* **Shot Type:** High-Impact Webtoon Typographic Split
* **Visual Direction:** Split panel with extreme psychological contrast. 
  * Top: The leader boy's face drenched in cold sweat, teeth clenched, shouting with raw panic.
  * Bottom: Giant jagged red and white warning typography slashing across the darkness like a manhwa curse interface.
* **SFX:** *THUMP-THUMP! (Violent racing heartbeat)*
* **Dialogue (Leader Boy - HI):** 
  "कोई भी पीछे मत मुड़ना!
  अगर तुमने उसे मुड़कर देखा—तो तुम मरे!
  अगर तुमने उसका नाम लिया या सुना—तो तुम मरे!
  अगर तुमने उसके बारे में सोचा भी—तो तुम मरे!"
* **Dialogue (Leader Boy - EN):** 
  "Nobody turn around!
  If you look back—you die!
  If you speak or hear its name—you die!
  If you even think about it—you die!"

---

### [PROLOGUE BEAT 2: THE INEVITABLE CASUALTIES]
#### PANEL P04
* **Shot Type:** Medium Close-Up — The Breaking Point
* **Visual Direction:** A younger child (around 10 years old) in the middle of the line tears up, sobbing silently. Legs trembling uncontrollably, hands clutching his own chest, face pale with hopeless resignation.
* **SFX:** *SOB... WHIMPER...*
* **Dialogue (Crying Child - HI):** "हम नहीं भाग सकते! हम चाहे जितनी भी कोशिश कर लें... हम न वक्त बदल पा रहे हैं, न ही तकदीर। मुझे लगता है कि... हम सब मर जाएंगे।"
* **Dialogue (Crying Child - EN):** "We can't escape! No matter how hard we try... we can't change time, nor our fate. I think... we're all going to die."

---

#### PANEL P05
* **Shot Type:** Dramatic Close-Up Profile — Fierce Will to Survive
* **Visual Direction:** The teenage leader turns his head just an inch to the side (without glancing back). His dark eyes blaze with fierce, desperate pragmatism under his damp bangs.
* **Dialogue (Leader Boy - HI):** "किसने कहा कि हम बचने की कोशिश कर रहे हैं? हम तो बस खुद के लिए वक्त चुरा रहे हैं... जितना चुरा सकते हैं, उतना चुरा रहे हैं!"
* **Dialogue (Leader Boy - EN):** "Who said we're trying to escape? We're just stealing time for ourselves... as much time as we can possibly steal!"

---

#### PANEL P06
* **Shot Type:** Multi-Tiered Psychological Horror Montage
* **Visual Direction:** 
  * Top tier: A child accidentally glances back in panic—instantly wrapped in black smoky tendrils.
  * Middle tier: Another child covers his ears with bloodshot eyes, screaming as ghostly whispers penetrate his mind.
  * Bottom tier: A child clutches his temples, collapsing into the darkness as the shadow horde engulfs them one by one.
* **Caption (Narrator - HI):** *वे चाहकर भी इन गलतियों से बच नहीं पा रहे थे... कोई उन्हें देख ले रहा था, तो कोई सुन ले रहा था। इसलिए वे धीरे-धीरे गिरते और उनके आगोश में समाते जा रहे थे।*
* **Caption (Narrator - EN):** *Try as they might, the slip-ups were fatal... one caught a glimpse, another heard a whisper. One by one, they fell into the dark entity's suffocating embrace.*

---

### [PROLOGUE BEAT 3: THE LAST STAND & MUTILATION GAMBIT]
#### PANEL P07
* **Shot Type:** Lone Survivor Wide Angle — The Broken Defiance
* **Visual Direction:** The teenage leader is now the only one left standing on the empty dirt road. Towering black silhouettes rise right behind his heels. In sheer madness and instinctual survival fury, he drops to one knee and scoops up a heavy, jagged roadside stone with both shaking hands.
* **SFX:** *CLACK! (Stone lifted)*
* **Dialogue (Leader Boy - HI):** "तो ठीक है! अगर मेरी 2 मिनट की ज़िंदगी के लिए भी मेरे आँख-कान ही रोड़े बन रहे हैं... तो मैं उन्हें ही नष्ट कर दूँगा!"
* **Dialogue (Leader Boy - EN):** "Fine then! If my own eyes and ears are the roadblocks to two more minutes of life... I'll destroy them myself!"

---

#### PANEL P08
* **Shot Type:** Visceral Horror Medium Close-Up
* **Visual Direction:** In sheer frantic terror, the boy violently strikes his own ear with the jagged stone. Blood splatters against the mud. His eyes are wide with madness and pain, hand trembling as he prepares to smash it again and again to deafen himself before the shadows strike.
* **SFX:** *THUD! CRACK!*

---

#### PANEL P09
* **Shot Type:** Extreme Close-Up — The Frozen Hand
* **Visual Direction:** Realizing bludgeoning his ears won't save him in time, the boy raises the bloody stone directly toward his own eyes. But inches before his face, dark, smoke-like shadowy claws emerge from behind and clamp tightly around his wrists, freezing his arm dead in mid-air.
* **SFX:** *SHHHK! (Ghostly grip)*

---

#### PANEL P10
* **Shot Type:** Melancholic Atmospheric Close-Up — The Final Glance
* **Visual Direction:** The stone falls from limp fingers into the dirt. The boy's eyes mist with tears as the black shadows begin wrapping around his shoulders and neck. Looking through the mist, he sees the blurred silhouettes of his village huts, thatched roofs, and the road where he grew up—a quiet, heartbreaking farewell to his lost childhood.
* **Caption (Narrator - HI):** *यह शायद उसकी आखिरी कोशिश थी, जो वह हार चुका था... मन मारकर अब उसने खुद को उनके रहमों-करम पर छोड़ दिया। वह अपने बचपन, घर और इलाके को अंतिम बार देख रहा था।*
* **Caption (Narrator - EN):** *It was his final gamble, and he had lost... with a heavy heart, he surrendered to their mercy, gazing upon his village and childhood for the very last time.*

---

### [PROLOGUE BEAT 4: THE TORCHBEARER & THE DYING PLEA]
#### PANEL P11
* **Shot Type:** Atmospheric Wide Composition — The Lone Traveler
* **Visual Direction:** Across the distant village road on the high ridge, an unknown solitary traveler strides steadily through the midnight mist. He carries a blazing torch that casts warm, golden embers into the dark. He walks with calm, unbothered authority, unaffected by the encroaching shadows—as though a silent pact of mutual indifference exists between them.
* **SFX:** *CRACKLE... FSSSH... (Blazing torch flame)*

---

#### PANEL P12
* **Shot Type:** Emotional Hero Beat / The Desperate Cry
* **Visual Direction:** Extreme close-up of the leader boy being pulled down into the swirling shadow vortex. Drenched in tears and blood, he uses the last breath in his lungs to cry out toward the distant golden torchlight, hands folded together in a desperate plea.
* **Dialogue (Leader Boy - HI):** 
  "कौन हो तुम?! क्या तुम दोस्त हो?! क्या हमें बचाने आए हो?! 
  यह जगह बहुत खतरनाक है... तुम चले जाओ! वरना तुम भी...! 
  तुम मुझे नहीं बचा पाओगे। पर जाने से पहले मेरा एक काम कर सकते हो? 
  अगर मेरे दोस्तों को बचा सकते हो तो प्लीज उन्हें बचा लो! मैं हाथ जोड़ता हूँ... उन्हें बचा लो!"
* **Dialogue (Leader Boy - EN):** 
  "Who are you?! Are you a friend?! Did you come to save us?! 
  This place is too dangerous... run away! Or else you will also...! 
  You can't save me. But before you leave, can you do one thing for me? 
  If you can save my friends, please save them! I beg of you... save them!"

---

#### PANEL P13
* **Shot Type:** Sudden Halt / The Cliffhanger Hook
* **Visual Direction:** Cinematic wide low-angle focusing on the mysterious traveler with the blazing torch. Amidst the howling wind and darkness, the faint, desperate cry reaches his ear. The traveler's boots halt instantly on the dirt path. His silhouette stands rigid as a statue, surprised. Slowly, deliberately, his head begins to turn back toward the dark abyss...
* **SFX:** *STEP... HALT!*
* **Caption (Narrator - HI):** *उसने आवाज के स्त्रोत को जानने के लिए उस ओर गर्दन मोड़नी शुरू कर दी... पर...*
* **Caption (Narrator - EN):** *To locate the source of the voice, he slowly began to turn his head back... but...*
* **Bottom Banner:** **[ PROLOGUE COMPLETE — THE CURSE AWAKENS ]**

## SCENE 1: THE HAVELI FORTRESS — INNER SANCTUM (PRESENT)
**Setting:** Dynamite Anna's grand, decaying British Colonial/Zamindari mansion. Night. Rain drizzling outside, harsh yellow sodium lamps cutting through shadows.

### [HERO BEAT 1: ESTABLISHING SHOT]
#### PANEL 1
* **Shot Type:** Wide Exterior Establishing Shot (Vertical Scroll Bleed)
* **Visual Direction:** The imposing, decaying heritage mansion compound surrounded by high stone walls topped with razor barbed wire. Armed mercenaries in combat fatigues with automatic rifles patrol the balconies and watchtowers like a military cantonment. Black vintage luxury sedans parked on wet gravel. Rain drizzle falling through harsh yellow sodium spotlights.
* **SFX:** *SHSHSHSH... (Heavy rain pattering)*
* **Caption (Narrator - EN):** *An opulent palace where the walls had grown ancient and cracked... guarded by an army ready to die at a single finger's gesture.*
* **Caption (Narrator - HI):** *एक आलीशान महल जहाँ की दीवारें ज़रा पुरानी हो गई थीं... आस-पास बंदूकधारियों की ऐसी फ़ौज खड़ी थी, जो एक इशारे पर मर मिटने को तैयार थे।*

---

#### PANEL 2
* **Shot Type:** Interior Wide Angle — Low Lighting
* **Visual Direction:** A dimly lit, cavernous drawing room inside the fortress. Antique Persian rugs, peeling lime-washed pillars. In the corner, **Dynamite Anna (Neelkanth Sethupathi)** reclines in a deep red velvet armchair with one leg crossed over the other. Half-buttoned cream silk shirt revealing a thick solid gold chain across his heavy chest. Crisp white dhoti with gold zari border. Dark round sunglasses concealing his eyes despite the low light. Thick curved handlebar mustache. He holds a smoldering Cuban cigar between two thick fingers.
* **SFX:** *FSSSHHH... (Cigar smoke curling)*
* **Caption (EN):** *The chieftain of the syndicate was lounging in a plush chair, savoring expensive cigars. He wore dark glasses indoors as if allergic to sunlight.*
* **Caption (HI):** *उनके दल का मुखिया एक मुलायमदार कुर्सी में बैठ महँगी सिगारों का आनंद ले रहा था। कमरे में रोशनी न होने पर भी आँखों पर काला चश्मा चढ़ा हुआ था।*

---

#### PANEL 3
* **Shot Type:** Over-the-Shoulder Shot (From behind Anna looking at Rahul)
* **Visual Direction:** In front of Anna stands **Rahul**. He looks hollow-cheeked, visibly starved, slender frame lost inside a worn olive utility jacket with rolled-up cuffs. Disheveled black bangs falling across his forehead, dark bags under his eyes. Despite his emaciation, his bone structure shows he comes from a respectable family. On a low brass coffee table between them sits an opened velvet pouch displaying glittering imitation gold ornaments.
* **SFX:** *TICK... TICK... (Cracked wristwatch ticking on Rahul's wrist)*
* **Caption (EN):** *Before him stood a young man, shivering with dread. Gaunt and frail, as if he hadn't eaten in weeks... yet his face carried the quiet dignity of a good home fallen on dark days.*
* **Caption (HI):** *उसके सामने एक नौजवान था, जो बड़ा डरा-सहमा हुआ था। दिखने में बेहद दुबला-पतला, मानों हफ़्तों से भूखा हो... पर चेहरे से किसी भले घराने का लग रहा था, बस मुसीबतों का मारा हुआ।*

---

#### PANEL 4
* **Shot Type:** Medium Close-Up — Anna's Face
* **Visual Direction:** Anna slowly exhales a thick ring of grey cigar smoke into the air. Through the dark lenses, his expression is bored and unreadable. He raises two fingers.
* **Dialogue (Anna - EN):** "Six lakhs."
* **Dialogue (Anna - HI):** "6 लाख।"

---

#### PANEL 5
* **Shot Type:** Close-Up — Rahul's Eyes & Expression
* **Visual Direction:** Rahul's eyes widen with sudden, overwhelming hope and excitement. He leans forward, his hands trembling with naive honesty.
* **Dialogue (Rahul - EN):** "What?! You'll give me six lakhs for these?! But... why should I lie to you? These ornaments are barely worth ten thousand! They only have a thin layer of gold plating over cheap brass!"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - HI):** "क्या बात कर रहे हैं!! इसके आप मुझे 6 लाख देंगे? पर झूठ क्यों बोलूँ! गहने तो बस 10 हज़ार के ही हैं। यह जेवर सोने के दिख तो रहे हैं, पर हैं पीतल के, सोने का पानी चढ़ा हुआ है बस।"

---

### [HERO BEAT 2: THE CRUEL NEGOTIATION]
#### PANEL 6
* **Shot Type:** Dramatic Wide Low-Angle (Tension Climax)
* **Visual Direction:** Anna lets out a dry, rumbling chuckle. Behind his chair, **Jaleel** (Anna's lean, wiry enforcer with an undercut hairstyle and a razor scar on his cheek) steps forward, sneering with cruel amusement. Jaleel taps his finger against his own temple, mocking Rahul.
* **SFX:** *HAHAHAHA!*
* **Dialogue (Jaleel - EN):** "You idiot! Anna isn't giving *you* six lakhs—*you* have to give *us* six lakhs! Did you forget? You owe us 6.10 lakhs! We're confiscating these ten-thousand-rupee trinkets, but you better arrange the remaining six lakhs fast. Because the day we come to collect at your door, your doom arrives with us!"
* **Dialogue (Jaleel - HI):** "अबे 6 लाख हमको नहीं, तुझको देना है, भूल गया तू? 6.10 लाख का उधार है हमारा तुझपर! यह 10 हज़ार के गहने तो हम रख रहे हैं, पर बाक़ी के पैसों का इंतज़ाम भी जल्दी कर वरना जिस दिन हम अपने पैसे वसूलने तेरे घर आए, हमारे साथ तेरी शामत भी आएगी!"

---

#### PANEL 7
* **Shot Type:** Medium Shot — Rahul Pleading
* **Visual Direction:** The color drains from Rahul's face. He joins his hands in desperation, his voice cracking with emotion.
* **Dialogue (Rahul - EN):** "I know... but the whole city speaks of your generosity. My brother is lying in the hospital right now, hanging between life and death. If I am forced to sell my mother's jewelry, you can understand how grave the situation is!"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - HI):** "मैं जानता हूँ, पर आपकी दरियादिली के बारे में पूरा शहर जानता है। मेरा भाई अभी अस्पताल में ज़िंदगी और मौत के बीच झूल रहा है। अगर मुझे अपनी माँ के जेवर बेचने पड़ रहे हैं तो आप समझ ही सकते हैं कि मामला कितना गंभीर है।"

---

#### PANEL 8
* **Shot Type:** Extreme Close-Up — Anna's Lips & Cigar
* **Visual Direction:** Anna doesn't even turn his head. He takes another slow drag from his cigar and mutters coldly towards Jaleel.
* **Dialogue (Anna - EN):** "Get this sight out of my eyes."
* **Dialogue (Anna - HI):** "इसको अब हटाओ मेरी नज़रों से।"

---

#### PANEL 9
* **Shot Type:** Dynamic High-Angle Action Panel
* **Visual Direction:** Jaleel snaps into sudden, violent motion. He draws a black semi-automatic pistol from his leather shoulder holster and points the muzzle directly between Rahul's eyes, cocking the hammer.
* **SFX:** *CLACK! (Gun hammer cocked)*
* **Dialogue (Jaleel - EN):** "Get lost! Go cry your sob story somewhere else! Whether your brother lives or dies, what is it to us? That cop interfered with the Boss way too much back in the day—this is the karma for his sins! Let the bastard die!"
* **Dialogue (Jaleel - HI):** "बे जा! अपना रोना कहीं और जाकर सुना। निकल यहाँ से! तेरा भाई मरे चाहे जिये उससे हमको क्या? वैसे भी उस पुलिसिये ने बोस के ख़िलाफ़ अपने टाइम कितनी उंगली की थी। उसी पापों का नतीजा है वो। मरने दे साले को!"

---

#### PANEL 10
* **Shot Type:** Downward Long Shot — Silhouette
* **Visual Direction:** Rahul walking with slumped shoulders through the armed courtyard. Mercenaries laugh in the background. Rain drips from his hair.
* **Dialogue (Mercenary O.S. - EN):** *"Haha! That cop brother tried to become a roadblock in front of Anna, and the Boss moved a single finger—not only did he lose his badge, the bastard has a criminal case on himself now! Hahaha!"*
* **Dialogue (Mercenary O.S. - HI):** *"हाहाहा! इसका पुलिसिया भाई हमारे बोस के सामने रोड़े डाल रहा था, फिर बॉस ने ऐसी उंगली घुमाई कि नौकरी गई वो अलग, साले पर खुद केस चल रहा अब! हाहाहा!"*
* **Caption (Rahul Monologue - EN):** *My heart tore in my chest... but what power did I have? Swallowing the humiliation, I chose to walk out in silence.*
* **Caption (Rahul Monologue - HI):** *मन तो बहुत दुखी हुआ, पर अब मैं कर भी क्या सकता था? मन मारते हुए चुपचाप वहाँ से निकलने में ही भलाई समझी।*

## SCENE 2: THE GOVERNMENT HOSPITAL WARD (AFTERNOON)

* **Shot Type:** Medium Two-Shot

* **Dialogue (Brother - EN):** "You went to sell Mom's jewelry... did you manage to arrange some money?"
* **Dialogue (Brother - HI):** "माँ के जो गहने बेचने गए थे, कुछ पैसों का इंतज़ाम हुआ?"

* **Dialogue (Rahul - HI):** "हाँ! 6 लाख।"

---

#### PANEL 12
* **Shot Type:** Close-Up — Brother's Surprised Face
* **Visual Direction:** The brother's eyes widen in genuine astonishment.

* **Dialogue (Rahul - HI):** "वही तो बताकर आया हूँ! तभी तो 10 हज़ार काटकर, 6 लाख का उधार छोड़ दिया!"

---

#### PANEL 13

* **Visual Direction:**

  * Right: Brother giving a deadpan, affectionate older-brother grin from the pillow.

* **Dialogue (Brother - EN):** "I was listening clearly... but you were the one driving, Driver Sahab!"
* **Dialogue (Brother - HI):** "मैं सुन रहा था... पर गाड़ी तुम चला रहे थे, ड्राइवर साहब!"

---

#### PANEL 14
* **Shot Type:** Close-Up — Brother's Resilient Smile
* **Visual Direction:** Rahul drops his head into his hands, on the verge of breaking. The brother smiles gently with deep warmth.
