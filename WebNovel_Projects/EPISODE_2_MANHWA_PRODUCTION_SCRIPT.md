# Webtoon / Manhwa Production Script: Episode 2
**Series Working Title:** *The Panopticon: Dynamite City* (पैनोप्टिकॉन: बारूद का साया)  
**Episode 2 Title:** *An Odd Boy and the Interviewer* (अध्याय 2: एक अजीब सा लड़का)  
**Format:** Vertical Scroll Webtoon / Manhwa Standard  
**Visual Style Lock:** 2D Korean Webtoon Manhwa (*Solo Leveling* / *Castle* style, sharp cel-shaded linework, dynamic digital lighting, zero 3D CGI)  
**Visual Anchors:** Matched to `webnovel_assets/characters/` & `webnovel_assets/environments/`  
**Panel Count:** 22 High-Tension Panels  
**Climax Hook:** The shocking identity reveal—the goofy street victim is Rahul's interviewer!

---

## SCENE 1: THE BAZAAR & THE STONE BENCH
**Setting:** A bustling, claustrophobic morning market on Old Barracks Road. Fruit vendors, sputtering rickshaws, harsh morning sunlight cutting through dust.

#### PANEL 1
* **Shot Type:** Wide Establishing High-Angle (Vertical Scroll Bleed)
* **Visual Direction:** The chaotic morning market on Old Barracks Road. Vegetable carts, honking scooters, crowded pedestrians. Right in the middle stands a rundown two-story colonial brick building with peeling lime paint. On the ground floor, a single locked iron door stands flush against the pavement with a stone sitting ledge (पिंडा) beside it.
* **SFX:** *पीं-पीं... शोर-शराबा... (Bazaar street ambience)*
* **Caption (Rahul Monologue - HI):** "14 ओल्ड बैरक्स रोड। एक भीड़-भाड़ वाला बाज़ार... जहाँ हर रोज़ सैकड़ों चेहरे गुज़रते हैं, पर कोई किसी को नहीं देखता।"
* **Caption (Rahul Monologue - EN):** "14 Old Barracks Road. A crowded market... where hundreds of faces pass by each day, yet no one truly sees anyone."

---

#### PANEL 2
* **Shot Type:** Medium Low-Angle
* **Visual Direction:** Rahul sits on the cold stone ledge beside the dark iron door, hands buried deep inside his olive utility jacket. Taped to the weathered iron door is the exact promotional flyer from his house. The door handle is heavy, industrial, and locked from the inside.
* **SFX:** *टिक... टिक... (Hairline-cracked wristwatch ticking on Rahul's wrist)*
* **Caption (Rahul - HI):** "दरवाज़ा बंद था, शायद अंदर से। मैं बस एक-दो मिनट ही बैठा था कि..."
* **Caption (Rahul - EN):** "The door was locked, likely from inside. I had barely been seated for a minute or two when..."

---

## SCENE 2: THE GOOFY STREET BOY ARRIVES
**Setting:** Outside the locked iron door on Old Barracks Road.

#### PANEL 3
* **Shot Type:** Dynamic Full-Body Reveal Shot
* **Visual Direction:** A 17-18 year old boy walks briskly through the market crowd toward the door, clutching a crumpled flyer. It's **Gaurav**—messy curly black hair, bruised cheekbone, scuffed oversized pastel hoodie. He has refined, idol-like facial features, but slightly prominent front teeth that give him an endearing, goofy expression.
* **SFX:** *चप... चप... (Casual footsteps)*

---

#### PANEL 4
* **Shot Type:** Tight Double Reaction Split
* **Visual Direction:** 
  * Top: Rahul's eyes widening in instant recognition, eyebrows shooting up.
  * Bottom: Gaurav stopping dead in his tracks, his jaw dropping in comedic disbelief.
* **Dialogue (Rahul - HI):** "अरे! तुम तो वही कल वाले लड़के हो! यहाँ क्या कर रहे हो?"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - EN):** "Hey! You're the kid from yesterday! What are you doing here?"

---

#### PANEL 5
* **Shot Type:** Medium Two-Shot — Street Banter
* **Visual Direction:** Gaurav blinks as if waking from a daydream, then points an excited, accusatory finger right at Rahul's face with a wide grin.
* **SFX:** *हाहा!*
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - HI):** "सिगरेट वाला लड़का! तुम वही हो न जो कल उल्टा सिगरेट पी रहा था और कुत्ते की तरह भौंक रहा था?!"
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - EN):** "Cigarette boy! You're the guy who was smoking backward and barking like a dog yesterday?!"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - HI):** "और तुम वही न, जो अन्ना के आदमियों से मार खाने जाता है और खून निकलने तक वहीं बैठ जाता है?!"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - EN):** "And you're the guy who goes out to get beaten by Anna's men and sits there till you bleed?!"
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - HI):** "हाँ! मैं वही हूँ!"
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - EN):** "Yeah! That's me!"

---

#### PANEL 6
* **Shot Type:** Low-Angle Side-by-Side on the Ledge
* **Visual Direction:** Gaurav casually plops down on the stone ledge right beside Rahul, swinging his legs with carefree energy.
* **Dialogue (Rahul - HI):** "यहाँ क्या कर रहे हो?"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - EN):** "What are you doing here?"
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - HI):** "इश्तेहार देखा—बोले ड्राइवर की पोस्ट के लिए इंटरव्यू है, तो चला आया। तुम भी इसीलिए आए हो?"
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - EN):** "Saw the flyer—said there's an interview for a driver's post, so I came. You here for that too?"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - HI):** "हाँ। पर... तुम्हें ड्राइविंग आती भी है?"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - EN):** "Yeah. But... do you even know how to drive?"
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - HI):** "नहीं आती! पर कर लूँगा, उसमें क्या! पैसे चाहिए, तो करनी ही पड़ेगी!"
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - EN):** "Nope! But I'll learn, what's the big deal! Need money, so gotta do it!"

---

#### PANEL 7
* **Shot Type:** Close-Up — Gaurav's Audacious Request
* **Visual Direction:** Gaurav leans close to Rahul, making a comical, earnest pleading face with clasped hands.
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - HI):** "देखो, अगर तुम्हें सच में मुझसे हमदर्दी है तो एक काम कर सकते हो? यहाँ से चले जाओ! इस नौकरी के लिए बस हम दो ही आए हैं। अगर दोनों का इंटरव्यू हुआ, तो वे पक्का तुम्हें ही चुनेंगे!"
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - EN):** "Look, if you genuinely feel bad for me, can you do one favor? Just leave! Only the two of us showed up for this job. If both of us interview, they'll definitely pick you!"

---

## SCENE 3: THE PANOPTICON DEDUCTION
**Setting:** Outside the locked door. Tension building.

#### PANEL 8
* **Shot Type:** Dramatic Over-the-Shoulder — Rahul's Serious Eyes
* **Visual Direction:** Rahul exhales slowly, his tone turning serious and dark. Gaurav's goofy expression freezes.
* **Dialogue (Rahul - HI):** "गौरव, अगर मुझे तुम्हारी परवाह है, तो मुझे यहाँ से बिल्कुल नहीं जाना चाहिए।"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - EN):** "Gaurav, if I care about you, leaving is the last thing I should do."
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - HI):** "क्यों भला?"
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - EN):** "Why's that?"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - HI):** "सोचो ज़रा—ड्राइवर की इतनी अच्छी पोस्ट के लिए सिर्फ हम दोनों ही क्यों आए? कोई अनुभवी ड्राइवर क्यों नहीं आया? और इस पोस्टर पर इंटरव्यू का कोई समय क्यों नहीं लिखा था?"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - EN):** "Think for a second—for such a lucrative driving post, why did only the two of us show up? Why no experienced drivers? And why was there no time mentioned on this flyer?"

---

#### PANEL 9
* **Shot Type:** Macro Psychological Close-Up — Rahul's Whispered Truth
* **Visual Direction:** Rahul leans in, speaking directly into Gaurav's ear. Shadows cut across Rahul's face, highlighting the paranoia and sharp intelligence beneath his tired exterior.
* **Dialogue (Rahul - HI):** "इन्होंने समय इसलिए नहीं लिखा, क्योंकि इन्हें उसकी ज़रूरत ही नहीं थी। हम जब भी आएँ, वे तैयार रहते... क्योंकि वे हम पर नज़र रख रहे हैं। कम से कम मुझ पर तो रख रहे हैं।"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - EN):** "They didn't write a time because they had no need for one. Whenever we arrived, they'd be ready... because they are watching us. At least, they're watching me."

---

#### PANEL 10
* **Shot Type:** Sudden Mechanical Shock
* **Visual Direction:** Right behind their heads, the heavy iron door emits a loud, industrial hydraulic click. The latch snaps open with a hiss of releasing air pressure. A synthetic, emotionless electronic voice chimes from an overhead grill.
* **SFX:** *खटाक... फस्सस! (CLANG-FSSSH!)*
* **Voice (Speaker - HI):** "[ अगला उम्मीदवार... अंदर आए। ]"
* **Voice (Speaker - EN):** "[ Next candidate... Enter. ]"

---

## SCENE 4: THE 5-MINUTE PACT & THE WAITING GAME
**Setting:** Outside the opened threshold. The interior is pitch black.

#### PANEL 11
* **Shot Type:** High-Angle Tension Two-Shot
* **Visual Direction:** Gaurav stands up abruptly. He looks toward the dark doorway, suddenly nervous, his playful demeanor cracking. He turns back to Rahul with wide, hesitant eyes.
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - HI):** "क्या तुम जो कह रहे थे... वो सच है? कि वे हम पर नज़र रखते हैं?"
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - EN):** "What you said just now... is it really true? That they watch us?"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - HI):** "व्यक्तिगत अनुभव से कहूँ तो... सौ प्रतिशत सच है।"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - EN):** "Speaking from personal experience... one hundred percent true."

---

#### PANEL 12
* **Shot Type:** Cinematic Medium Close-Up — The Handshake / Pact
* **Visual Direction:** Gaurav swallows hard. Standing at the threshold of the dark doorway, he looks at Rahul with genuine vulnerability.
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - HI):** "अगर मैं 5 मिनट में वापस नहीं आया... तो तुम मेरी मदद के लिए अंदर आ जाना।"
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - EN):** "If I don't come out in five minutes... come inside to help me."
* **Dialogue (Rahul - HI):** "ठीक है। 5 मिनट।"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - EN):** "Deal. Five minutes."

---

#### PANEL 13
* **Shot Type:** Sudden Dark Slam
* **Visual Direction:** Gaurav steps inside the dark room. The heavy iron door instantly slams shut with a terrifying, solid thud!
* **SFX:** *धड़ाम!! (SLAM!!)*

---

#### PANEL 14
* **Shot Type:** Time-Lapse Montage Panel (The 15-Minute Agony)
* **Visual Direction:** Split vertical slices showing Rahul waiting on the stone ledge.
  * Top: 5 minutes passed—Rahul checking his cracked wristwatch nervously.
  * Middle: 10 minutes passed—Rahul pacing back and forth, biting his thumbnail, sweat trickling down his cheek.
  * Bottom: 15 minutes passed—Rahul clenching his fists, staring at the dead silent iron door in rising dread.
* **SFX:** *धक-धक... धक-धक... (Racing heartbeat)*
* **Caption (Rahul - HI):** "पाँच मिनट कब पंद्रह मिनट में बदल गए, पता ही नहीं चला... अंदर से न कोई चीख आई, न कोई आवाज़।"
* **Caption (Rahul - EN):** "Five minutes turned into fifteen before I knew it... not a scream from inside, not a single sound."

---

## SCENE 5: THE WHITE VOID
**Setting:** Inside the room. Blinding sterile white light.

#### PANEL 15
* **Shot Type:** Sudden Kinetic Threshold Burst
* **Visual Direction:** The iron door suddenly unlocks with a sharp buzz. Rahul doesn't wait—he lunges forward with full force, shoving the heavy iron door open, bracing himself for a bloodbath or an ambush!
* **SFX:** *धड़ाक! (BANG!)*

---

#### PANEL 16
* **Shot Type:** Full-Bleed Blinding White Splash Panel
* **Visual Direction:** Rahul bursts inside and freezes in total shock. There is no blood, no syndicate hitmen, no weapons. The room is vast, stark, and bathed in blinding, sterile fluorescent white light. Peeling white walls, zero windows. In the dead center sits a single metal desk with a glowing computer monitor.
* **Visual Atmosphere:** Eerie, clinical, absolute dead silence.

---

#### PANEL 17
* **Shot Type:** Low-Angle Interior Wide
* **Visual Direction:** Sitting casually behind the glowing computer monitor in an ergonomic office chair is **Gaurav**! He is completely unharmed, one leg crossed over the other, typing casually on the keyboard. He looks up and greets Rahul with a bright, cheerful grin.
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - HI):** "अरे! तुम बड़े लेट हो गए! तुमने तो कहा था जल्दी आ जाओगे!"
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - EN):** "Hey! You're super late! You said you'd come right away!"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - HI):** "गौरव?! तुम... तुम ठीक हो?! तुम्हारा इंटरव्यू कैसा गया?!"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - EN):** "Gaurav?! You're... you're okay?! How did your interview go?!"

---

## SCENE 6: THE BIZARRE ANNOUNCEMENT
**Setting:** The white chamber, beside the glowing computer desk.

#### PANEL 18
* **Shot Type:** Medium Two-Shot
* **Visual Direction:** Gaurav leans back in the chair with an amused, satisfied chuckle.
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - HI):** "एकदम मस्त! यहाँ तक कि उन्होंने चुनाव भी कर लिया!"
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - EN):** "Totally awesome! In fact, they've already made their selection!"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - HI):** "वाह! मुबारक हो! उन्होंने तुम्हें चुन लिया?"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - EN):** "Whoa! Congratulations! They selected you?"
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - HI):** "मुझे? नहीं-नहीं! असल में उन्होंने तुम्हें चुना है!"
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - EN):** "Me? No, no! Actually... they selected YOU!"

---

#### PANEL 19
* **Shot Type:** Extreme Close-Up — Rahul's Total Confusion
* **Visual Direction:** Rahul's brow furrows in deep bewilderment. He glances around the empty white room, searching the bare corners.
* **Dialogue (Rahul - HI):** "मुझे?! यह कैसे हो सकता है? मैं तो अंदर तक नहीं आया! और वे लोग कौन हैं? यहाँ तो तुम्हारे सिवा कोई दिख तक नहीं रहा!"
* **Dialogue (Rahul - EN):** "Me?! How is that possible? I didn't even step inside! And who are 'they'? There's no one here except you!"

---

#### PANEL 20
* **Shot Type:** Over-the-Shoulder — The Laminated Document
* **Visual Direction:** Gaurav taps the desk. Sitting right in front of Rahul is a single printed dossier with official surveillance headers and timestamps. Gaurav points to it with a knowing smirk.
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - HI):** "पास आओ। कुर्सी पर बैठो और तुम्हारे आगे जो कागज़ रखा है... ज़रा उसे पढ़ो तो।"
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - EN):** "Come closer. Take a seat and read the paper lying right in front of you."

## SCENE 7: THE TWIST & THE CLIFFHANGER
**Setting:** The computer desk under harsh fluorescent glare.

#### PANEL 21
* **Shot Type:** Tight Macro Split — Reading the Dossier
* **Visual Direction:** Rahul quickly reads the dossier. His eyes track across high-res surveillance logs, photos of him walking down the street, and CCTV logs of his apartment. Gaurav watches Rahul's expression intently, his playful boyish aura evaporating into cold, chilling psychological dominance.
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - HI):** "वैसे... तुम यहाँ CCTV रिकॉर्डिंग और जासूसी की पड़ताल के लिए ही आए हो न? यह जानने कि भला तुम पर नज़र क्यों रखी जा रही है..."
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - EN):** "By the way... you came here investigating CCTV recordings and surveillance, didn't you? To find out why you're being watched..."

---

* **Visual Direction:** The realization strikes Rahul like a bolt of lightning. The paper trembles in his hand. Rahul jerks his head up, his dark eyes wide in sheer, breathless shock as he stares at this 17-year-old kid. Gaurav leans forward on his elbows, resting his chin on his hands, flashing an eerie, triumphant, razor-sharp smile.
* **Dialogue (Rahul - HI):**

  **"So... YOU are my interviewer?!**
  **You're the one conducting my test?!"**
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - HI):** 
  **"बिंगो!"**
* **Dialogue (Gaurav - EN):**
