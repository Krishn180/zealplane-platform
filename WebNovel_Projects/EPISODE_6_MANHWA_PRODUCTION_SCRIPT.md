# Webtoon / Manhwa Production Script: Episode 6
**Series Working Title:** *The Panopticon: Dynamite City* (पैनोप्टिकॉन: बारूद का साया)  
**Episode 6 Title:** *The Awakening & The Driving Test* (अध्याय 6: अगली सुबह, साया और 'द ड्राइविंग टेस्ट')  
**Format:** Vertical Scroll Webtoon / Manhwa Standard (Castle / Plaza Wars Pacing)  
**Visual Style Lock:** 2D Korean Webtoon Manhwa (*Castle* by Jeong Yeon, crisp G-pen inking, high-contrast `#000000` pitch blacks, 2-tone matte cel-shading, desaturated gritty noir palette)  
**Visual Anchors:**
* **Rahul:** Pale, battered, haunted dark circles under bloodshot eyes, worn utility jacket. Lingering neurotoxin trauma.
* **Inspector Pattanaik:** Late 30s. Elite supercop. Crisp white formal shirt, tailored trousers, neat black mustache, sharp jawline, predatory intelligence.
* **The Shadow Entity:** Uncanny towering faceless silhouette, fluid shadows.
* **The Panopticon Portal:** Sterile minimalist dark-web UI, cold white `#ffffff` and blood-red `#ef4444`.  
**Panel Count:** 24 High-Tension Narrative Panels  
**Climax Hook:** The terrifying contract terms of "The Driving Test" and the fresh crimson message on the wall: *"वह ज़िंदा है"*.

---

* **Visual Direction:** The reader sees through Rahul’s disoriented eyes. The room spins with migraine static lines. An old ceiling fan rotates lazily above. In the blurry doorway stand two uniformed police constables with service rifles. Seated directly in front of the bed on a simple wooden chair is a sharp, composed figure.

## SCENE 4: THE CONTRACT — "THE DRIVING TEST"
**Setting:** Rahul's battered wooden desk. The eerie glow of his old laptop screen.

#### PANEL 24

#### PANEL 25

* **Visual Direction:** The sterile white screen suddenly spasms with violent digital static. Heavy black geometric boxes slam onto the center of the display, revealing the ominous heading:

  > # **TASK SELECTION: "THE DRIVING TEST"**  
  > ### **(द ड्राइविंग टेस्ट)**
